Position géographique

Votre position est utilisée uniquement lorsque vous l'autorisez. Les heures de prière et la qibla sont calculées directement sur votre téléphone. Pour la recherche de mosquées, des coordonnées approximatives (arrondies à environ 1 km) sont envoyées, sans aucun identifiant personnel, au service public OpenStreetMap Overpass : overpass-api.de (Allemagne) ou, s'il ne répond pas, l'un de ses serveurs miroirs publics (overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems). Votre position n'est jamais enregistrée sur nos serveurs.

Paiements

Les paiements sont traités exclusivement par Apple (App Store) ou Google (Google Play). Nous n'avons jamais accès à vos coordonnées bancaires.

Ce que nous ne faisons pas

Aucune publicité, aucun traceur publicitaire, aucune revente de données. Les notifications sont générées localement sur votre appareil.

1. Controller

2. Data we collect

  • Email and account ID (including the identity shared by Apple or Google when you sign in with them): to create and secure your account. Stored with Supabase (EU servers).
  • Preferred language: to show the app in your language.
  • Subscription status and purchase history, linked to your account ID: to unlock Premium. Processed by RevenueCat, Apple and Google.
  • Location, only with your permission. Prayer times and qibla are computed on your device. For the mosque search, approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) are sent with no personal identifier to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service: overpass-api.de (Germany) or, when it doesn't answer, one of its public mirrors (overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems). Your location is never stored on our servers.
  • Bookmarks, settings and reminders: stored on your device only.

Payments are handled by Apple or Google only. We never see your card details. No ads, no ad tracking, no data selling. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

3. Legal bases

Performance of the contract (account, subscription) and your consent (location, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time in your phone settings.

4. Processors

Supabase (auth and database, EU hosting), RevenueCat (USA, under EU Standard Contractual Clauses), Apple and Google.

5. Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete it. Billing records may be kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required by law.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to or restrict its processing. Email contact@prepify.fr. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

7. Account deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. See also this page.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
